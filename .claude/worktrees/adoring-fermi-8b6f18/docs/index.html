Uplift modeling · randomized experiments

Who should get the email?

A retailer emailed its recent customers at random. A third got an email featuring men's merchandise, a third got one featuring women's merchandise, and a third got nothing. Because the groups were random, the data can show whose behavior the email changed, not just who was going to buy anyway. A replication on Criteo's much larger ad experiments asks the same questions with a test big enough to answer them. Set the economics, pick a budget, and check the answers yourself.
